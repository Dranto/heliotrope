# Monkey cries

Short clips for the Options' "Monkey cries" easter egg (design doc §6.3), cut from recordings on
Wikimedia Commons, high-passed, denoised, trimmed, normalised and re-encoded as mono MP3. Each clip
keeps the licence of its source recording.
